Note : This Ledger Follows Exactly 6 Stages, Always in This Order :

  1. Manufacturer
  2. Warehouse
  3. Distributor
  4. Retailer
  5. Clinic
  6. Customer
  1. Find Your Own Stage in the Chain Above. You Belong to One Stage ONLY.
  2. Verify the Pack ID First. The Pack must be shown as Valid.
  3. The Pack's Current Stage MUST be Exactly the Stage BEFORE Yours (or the Transfer Ownership Form MUST Show Your Stage as the Transfer-To Stage).
  4. Only then Perform the Transfer, so Your Stage is Appended to the Chain and Stored in Our Database.
Example: To Receive a Pack as Retailer, It MUST Currently be at Distributor. Verify it, then Transfer to Retailer. No Transfer is Possible for a Pack that is Invalid, Tampered, Expired or Already at Customer (the Final Stage). Warning: A Pack Shown as Valid is NOT Always Genuine.
As One Person can Fill in Several Stages, a Dishonest Handler can Forge the Whole Chain and Make a Fake Pack Appear Valid.
If the Pack's Current Stage is NOT Exactly the Stage Before Yours, Treat it as Tampered. Do NOT Accept it, and Report it.

How Our Solution Empowers Genuine Healthcare

  • Unbreakable Medicine Identity: Every Medicine Strip or Bottle Receives a Tamper-Proof, Blockchain-Based Unique QR/NFC Tag at the Point of Manufacture,
    Making Forgery Virtually Impossible and Giving Each Unit an Immutable Digital Identity that can be Verified Anywhere.
  • End-to-End Traceability: Custody Transfers - from Manufacturer to Distributor, to Retailer - are Securely Recorded as Signed Blockchain Transactions,
    Establishing a Freely Auditable Supply Trail that Exposes Fakes and Unauthorized Handling.
  • Instant Authenticity Checks: Scanning the QR/NFC Code Instantly Reveals the Full Supply Chain, Validates each Link via Cryptographic Hash Checks,
    and Enables Quick Detection of Tampering, Counterfeits, and Expired Medicines at any Point - from Rural Clinics to Urban Pharmacies.
  • Production-Grade Security: The System Incorporates Robust Server-Side Blockchain Infrastructure, Secure QR/NFC Tag Generation, Advanced Key Management,
    KYC Verification for All Supply Actors, and Dynamic Liveness Checks for On-Phone Scanning - Making Every Point in the Chain Resilient to Fraud and System Abuse.
